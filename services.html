SPOTLIGHT · DIGITAL MARKETING SERVICES29

How an engagement runs

  1. 1Start with a clear client brief

    You share your brand, objective, audience, campaign brief and budget. Spotlight turns these inputs into a coordinated strategy and content calendar.

  2. 2Create, approve and activate

    We develop copy and creative, get human approval, schedule and publish, and run paid amplification. AI-written copy stays a draft until reviewed, and budget changes start as recommendations that need human approval.

  3. 3Measure, learn and improve

    We monitor audience response and campaign performance, find the themes, formats, hooks and channels that work, and feed those learnings into next month's strategy: a closed learning loop across content and paid media.
