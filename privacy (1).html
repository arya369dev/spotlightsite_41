Privacy policy

How Spotlight collects, uses and protects your personal data. Last updated 6 October 2026.

Plain-language summary: we only collect what you give us in the audit form, plus analytics and ad-measurement cookies if you say yes. We never sell your data. You can ask us to see, correct or delete it at any time.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Spotlight ("Spotlight", "we", "us"), [registered company name], [registered address], India. For anything about your data, email hello@spotlight.agency.

We act as the data fiduciary (controller) for personal data collected through this website, under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and, where it applies to you, the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR").

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Audit form: name, email, phone (optional), website or social handle (optional), the service you're interested in, and your message.
  • Emails and event registrations: whatever you choose to send us.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device and pages visited, used by our hosting provider to deliver and secure the site.
  • Campaign data: if you arrive from an ad, the campaign tags in the link (for example utm_source or fbclid), so we know which ads work.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: only with your consent, see section 7.
  • Brand scanner: the website address you enter. Our server reads that public homepage once to show you the results; we don't store the address or the results.
  • SPOT·AI chat and voice: the questions you type or speak. Voice uses your browser's built-in speech features: spoken replies are generated on your device, and speech-to-text may be processed by your browser provider (for example Google in Chrome). If live AI is switched on, your typed messages are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate a reply and are not used to train models. We don't keep chat history on our servers; it stays in your browser tab.

3. Why we use it

  • To prepare and send your free growth audit and reply to your enquiry (based on your consent and your request).
  • To understand how the website is used and improve it (analytics, with your consent).
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ads (marketing, with your consent).
  • To keep the website secure and prevent spam and abuse (our legitimate interest).
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers who process it on our behalf and under contract:

  • Vercel — website hosting and delivery.
  • Automation and email tools (such as n8n and Resend) — to route your enquiry to our team.
  • Google Analytics — anonymous usage statistics, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Meta Platforms — ad measurement through the Meta Pixel and Conversions API, only if you accept marketing cookies. Contact details sent to Meta are hashed (encrypted one-way) first.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile — spam protection on forms, if enabled.
  • Anthropic — generates SPOT·AI chat replies, only if live AI is switched on.

Some providers may process data outside India. Where they do, we rely on their contractual and security safeguards.

5. How long we keep it

  • Audit and enquiry data: up to 24 months after our last contact, unless you become a client.
  • Analytics data: according to the retention settings of Google Analytics (we use 14 months).
  • Security logs: up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it;
  • withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing already done);
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity (DPDP Act);
  • if GDPR applies, object to or restrict processing and receive your data in a portable format.

Email hello@spotlight.agency. We reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can contact our Grievance Officer ([name], hello@spotlight.agency) and, after that, the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

7. Cookies

Nothing that tracks you loads until you choose. You can change your choice any time with .

  • Essential (always on): remember your cookie choice and whether you've seen the intro film. Stored in your browser's local and session storage.
  • Analytics (optional): Google Analytics 4 cookies such as _ga and _ga_*, up to 14 months.
  • Marketing (optional): Meta Pixel cookies such as _fbp and _fbc, up to 90 days.

8. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS with strict security headers. Form submissions are validated on our server, secret keys are never exposed in the browser, and access to enquiries is limited to our team.

9. Children

This website is for businesses and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

10. Changes

We'll update this page if our practices change and revise the date at the top.
