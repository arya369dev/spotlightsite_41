FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Still curious? Ask us anything in the audit form below.

What does Spotlight do?

Spotlight is an AI-powered media and digital marketing agency. We plan strategy, run Meta and Google ads, create social content and video, build brands and websites, and optimise for Google and AI search, all under one team.

What is included in the free growth audit?

Within 48 hours you receive a review of your website, ads, social presence and search visibility, a snapshot of what your top competitors are doing, and a prioritised list of the three changes we would make first. There is no obligation to hire us.

How does Spotlight use AI in marketing?

We use AI to research audiences and competitors, test many ad creatives in parallel, spot performance changes early, and draft content faster. Every strategy, creative and post is reviewed and approved by a person on our team before it goes live.

Do you only work with large brands?

No. We work with startups, growing D2C brands, local businesses and established companies. The audit tells us which services fit your stage and budget.

What is AI search optimisation (AEO / GEO)?

It is the work of making your brand easy for AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews to understand, trust and cite: clear structured data, question-led content, consistent facts across the web, and fast, crawlable pages.

How do we measure results?

We agree on business goals first, such as leads, sales or cost per acquisition, then track them in a live dashboard and send a plain-language report every week.
